## What to build

On an upgrade card the text runs over the rules and lines of the card. And the camp's screen and the between-runs screen stack everything in one tall column, so with many things on offer the screen is very tall and its symbols and lettering small: lay them out across the screen instead.

## Done when

- No text on any card touches a line of the card (looked at with the longest texts).
- The camp and between-runs screens use the width of the screen, and stay readable with everything on offer.
